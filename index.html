WHY WONDER WISP

Nature meets smart technology.

Wonder Wisp is designed to make plant care more responsible by combining several simple environmental ideas into one system.

💧

Smart Watering

The soil-moisture sensor helps determine whether watering is actually required, helping reduce unnecessary irrigation.

🌧️

Rainwater Reuse

Rainwater can be collected and stored in a reservoir, allowing available rainwater to be reused for gardening.

☀️

Solar Powered

Solar energy can provide renewable power for WonderWisp's low-energy electronic components.

♻️

Less Waste

Recycled materials and reusable components can support the environmental purpose of the product.

THE SYSTEM

Simple inside.
Smart outside.

Wonder Wisp connects its functions into a simple cycle designed around the needs of the plant.

01 — POWER
☀️

Solar Energy

Sunlight provides renewable energy for the system.

02 — SENSE
🌱

Check Soil

The sensor checks the moisture level of the soil.

03 — THINK
🧠

Smart Decision

The controller processes the sensor information.

04 — WATER
💦

Drip Irrigation

Water is delivered gradually near the plant.

05 — REUSE
🌧️

Rainwater

Stored rainwater can be used for future irrigation.

ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT

Designed for a greener tomorrow.

Wonder Wisp focuses on practical environmental benefits that can be tested and improved over time.

💧

Save Every Drop.

Smart moisture-based watering can help prevent unnecessary irrigation. Actual water savings should be measured through real testing.

🌍

Power the Future.

Using solar energy, reusing rainwater and considering recycled materials can help make plant care more environmentally responsible.

OUR VISION

Technology should protect nature.

Wonder Wisp is more than a plant-care device. It is an attempt to make every drop of water count, use renewable energy wherever possible, reduce unnecessary waste and encourage people to care for plants in a more responsible way.
