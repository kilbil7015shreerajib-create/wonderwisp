WHY WONDER WISP

Nature meets smart technology.

Wonder Wisp is designed to make plant care more responsible by combining several simple environmental ideas into one system.

💧

Smart Watering

The soil-moisture sensor helps determine whether watering is actually required, helping reduce unnecessary irrigation.

🌧️

Rainwater Reuse

Rainwater can be collected and stored in a reservoir, allowing available rainwater to be reused for gardening.

🔵

Bluetooth Connectivity

Wonder Wisp can connect to a nearby phone or compatible device through Bluetooth, allowing system information to be monitored wirelessly.

🔋

Not Solar Powered

Wonder Wisp does not rely on solar power. The electronic system can instead use a suitable battery or external electrical power source.

THE SYSTEM

Simple inside.
Smart outside.

Wonder Wisp connects its functions into a simple cycle designed around the needs of the plant.

01 — POWER
🔋

Power Supply

The system operates using a suitable battery or external electrical power source.

02 — SENSE
🌱

Check Soil

The sensor checks the moisture level of the soil.

03 — CONNECT
🔵

Bluetooth

Information can be communicated wirelessly to a nearby compatible device.

04 — WATER
💦

Drip Irrigation

05 — REUSE
🌧️

Rainwater

Stored rainwater can be used for future irrigation.

ENVIRONMENTAL IMPACT

Designed for a greener tomorrow.

Wonder Wisp focuses on practical environmental benefits that can be tested and improved over time.

💧

Save Every Drop.

Smart moisture-based watering can help prevent unnecessary irrigation. Actual water savings should be measured through real testing.

🔵

Stay Connected.

Bluetooth connectivity allows the system to communicate wirelessly with a compatible device, making plant-care information easier to monitor.

OUR VISION

Technology should protect nature.

Wonder Wisp is more than a plant-care device. It is an attempt to make every drop of water count, reuse rainwater, reduce unnecessary waste and encourage people to care for plants in a more responsible way. The system uses Bluetooth connectivity rather than depending on solar power.
